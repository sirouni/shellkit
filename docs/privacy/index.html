Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Local workspace

Terminal files, installed packages, and settings are stored in the app’s local storage. Deleting the app deletes its local data. Whether device backups retain this data depends on your system backup settings.

Collection and tracking

ShellKit does not include advertising tracking or developer-operated usage analytics, and does not automatically upload terminal files or command history to the developer. The built-in shell does not require a ShellKit account.

Network access and third-party tools

Package downloads, container image pulls, and network-enabled tools connect to the repositories and services you use. Those services may receive your IP address and request information. Prompts, code, files, and credentials you submit to AI tools are processed by the relevant provider under its own privacy policy. Review those policies before use.

Credentials and files

Credentials or tokens entered in the Linux environment may be stored in local files by the relevant tool. Take care when installing software, sharing files, or exporting logs. ShellKit does not automatically send these credentials to the developer.

Support requests

When you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send and use it to address your request. GitHub Issues are public; do not post passwords, API keys, or private files. To request deletion of support emails, contact sirouni@gmail.com, subject to information we must retain by law or to resolve disputes.

Website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and adds no analytics scripts or advertising cookies. GitHub may process technical access logs to provide hosting and security, under its privacy policy.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy as features change and will show the updated date here. Developer: Xiao Wang. Contact: sirouni@gmail.com.
